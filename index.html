About

I am a Research Scientist specializing in distributed systems, cloud engineering, data engineering, and scalable big data platforms. I received my PhD in Computer Science from The University of Melbourne, where I conducted my research at the Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems (CLOUDS) Laboratory under the supervision of Professor Rajkumar Buyya.

Following my PhD, I contributed to a joint research project between Qatar University and The University of Sydney, focusing on data placement strategies for tiered cloud-based storage services to optimize cost. I subsequently joined the Centre for Research on Engineering Software Technologies (CREST) at University of Adelaide as a Research Fellow. At CREST , I worked on a collaborative industry project supported by the Defence Science and Technology Group (DSTG). My research focused on the design and implementation of experimental platforms for evaluating distributed databases and big data frameworks, including Apache Spark and Apache Flink, across cloud, edge-cloud, and hybrid cloud environments using Microsoft Azure and OpenStack. The research investigated the performance, resource utilization, and energy efficiency of distributed data systems, contributing to both industrial reports and high-impact research publications.

My research has led to more than 20 peer-reviewed publications in leading international journals and conferences, with most of these publications as the lead author. I specialize in cloud computing, big data platforms, distributed databases, and modern data engineering ecosystems, with hands-on experience in Microsoft Azure, Apache Spark, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Hadoop, and NoSQL technologies. I am passionate about translating advanced research into practical industry solutions by designing scalable, efficient, and intelligent data-driven systems for next-generation cloud and distributed computing environments.
